    Enhancing Video Corpus Moment Retrieval in Long Ego-centric Videos with LLM and Audio Fusion

    Published in In preparation, 2026

    Finding the right moment inside hours of egocentric video by fusing audio with language-model reasoning over the visual stream.

    Status

    This work is under development — no preprint and no results to report yet.

    If it overlaps something you are working on, get in touch; I would rather compare notes early than collide at submission.
