## Related

Part of the same line of work as [Seeing Culture](/publications/seeing-culture/)
(EMNLP 2025) and the [Cultural Moment benchmark](/publications/cultural-moment/),
which ask whether a model reasons about a cultural artifact or only sounds
confident about it. This one pushes the grounding requirement from a bounding box
to a segmentation mask.
