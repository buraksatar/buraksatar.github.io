## Related

It continues the video corpus moment retrieval work I began during a research
visit to Dima Damen's group at the University of Bristol, and follows the
retrieval line of [Frame Length Bias](/publications/frame-length-bias/)
(BMVC 2023) and [An Overview of Challenges in Egocentric Text-Video
Retrieval](/publications/egocentric-challenges/) (CVPR 2023 Workshop) into
longer, untrimmed footage.
